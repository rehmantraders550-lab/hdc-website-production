START WITH THE APPLICATION

The exact glass guides the decision.

Glass type, coating or treatment, printable face, geometry, intended use and cleaning conditions can change the route. HDC reviews those details and defines any sample or validation step before confirming production scope.

  • Project inputsExact item or sample, glass type and supplier details if known, coating or treatment, dimensions, quantity, intended use and artwork.
  • Candidate methodsUV direct print, UV-DTF transfer or an alternative may be considered after the job is reviewed.
  • Review statusProject review required. This page does not indicate that a glass process is pre-approved.
