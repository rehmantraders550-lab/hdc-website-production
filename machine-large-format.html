WHAT THE SYSTEM DOES

Understand the machine before choosing the route.

Large-format digital systems produce graphics at display and environmental scale on approved roll or rigid media according to the installation brief.

  • Best-considered applicationsRetail displays, event graphics, signs, wall graphics, exhibition elements and other physical brand-environment applications.
  • Material / surface contextMedia and substrates selected according to installation method, location, surface, viewing distance and project scope.
  • Validation boundaryFinal material, finishing, mounting and installation requirements depend on the confirmed environment. Outdoor-life and durability claims are not made without a defined material system.
