PRODUCTION METHOD — 01

UV DTF Transfer

A transfer-based production method that may be considered for selected hard-surface label, decal, and object applications.

  • What it doesCreates a printed transfer on release film for later application to a selected finished object or hard surface. Final suitability depends on the actual surface and intended handling.
  • Best-considered applicationsSelected branded objects, hard-surface labels or decals, and decorative applications where transfer-based production is appropriate.
  • Material & surface contextSurface condition, coating, geometry, and intended handling all affect whether the method is appropriate.
  • Suitability & review factorsSurface compatibility, geometry, coating, artwork, and intended handling should be reviewed before production is confirmed. No universal suitability or adhesion claim is made.
  • Related servicesLabels & Decals, Products & Object Printing

PRODUCTION METHOD — 02

Direct-to-Object UV Printing

Direct-to-object UV printing, using a flatbed production route where appropriate, may be considered when a suitable rigid substrate or object can be physically accommodated and its surface is appropriate for the intended print application.

  • What it doesApplies UV-curable ink directly to the selected workpiece rather than using a separate transfer film.
  • Best-considered applicationsSelected rigid objects, panels, boards, or display substrates where direct printing is physically and technically appropriate.
  • Material & surface contextPhysical dimensions, surface condition, geometry, and any preparation requirements must be reviewed for the actual job.
  • Suitability & review factorsPhysical clearance, geometry, surface condition, artwork, and intended handling must be confirmed against the actual job.
  • Related servicesProducts & Object Printing, Large Format & Brand Environments

PRODUCTION METHOD — 03

Offset Lithography

A commercial sheetfed print method used for defined paper and board applications where format, stock, artwork, quantity, and finish requirements are established.

  • What it doesTransfers ink through the offset lithographic process onto sheetfed paper or board for suitable commercial print applications.
  • Best-considered applicationsPackaging, stationery, printed communication, and other commercial paper or board applications where offset production is appropriate.
  • Material & surface contextPaper and board selection is reviewed against the application, format, artwork, finishing requirements, and production plan.
  • Suitability & review factorsQuantity, sheet format, stock or board, artwork, finish requirements, and downstream production needs should be reviewed before release.
  • Related servicesPackaging & Commercial Print

PRODUCTION METHOD — 04

Large Format Digital Printing

Large-format digital production may be considered for displays, signage, and physical brand environments where dimensions, substrate, artwork, and project context are defined.

  • What it doesProduces large-format printed output on suitable media selected for the intended display or environmental application.
  • Best-considered applicationsDisplays, signage, retail or exhibition graphics, wall or window applications, and other physical brand-environment work within confirmed scope.
  • Material & surface contextMedia and substrate choices are reviewed according to the intended environment, dimensions, finish, installation, and project conditions.
  • Suitability & review factorsViewing conditions, dimensions, substrate, artwork, installation context, and intended use should be reviewed before the route is confirmed.
  • Related servicesLarge Format & Brand Environments

PRODUCTION METHOD — 05

DTF Textile Transfer

A transfer-based production method that may be considered for selected textile applications after the fabric, artwork, quantity, and intended use are reviewed.

  • What it doesCreates a printed transfer on film for later application to a suitable textile using the required transfer process.
  • Best-considered applicationsSelected textile applications where transfer-based decoration is appropriate. DTF remains a production method rather than a core HDC service family.
  • Material & surface contextFabric type, construction, surface, and heat sensitivity should be reviewed for the actual application.
  • Suitability & review factorsFabric characteristics, artwork, intended use, and production conditions should be reviewed before the method is confirmed. It is not a standalone core service family.
