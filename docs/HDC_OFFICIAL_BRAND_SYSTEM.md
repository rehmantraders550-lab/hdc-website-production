# HDC Official Brand System

**Status:** Team-approved / locked  
**Effective:** 2026-09-03  
**Website doctrine:** Engineered Tactility

## Authority

The supplied Hadi Digital Craft logo is the official logo and the governing colour source for the HDC website. The website must use the real logo asset rather than reconstructing the identity as text.

Taste Skill is integrated as a design-quality review dependency. It can challenge generic composition, weak hierarchy, poor responsive behaviour and unnecessary visual effects, but it cannot redefine HDC's logo, palette, typography doctrine, commercial-printing position, architecture or evidence rules.

## Usage doctrine

Black/charcoal and ivory remain the principal spatial fields. Teal/aqua carries technical interaction, active states, fine rules and controlled emphasis. Gold/copper carries tactile and material moments. Warm and cool accents should not be used at equal intensity everywhere; restraint is part of the identity.

Do not introduce unrelated lime, RGB-neon, generic SaaS gradients, fake futuristic lighting or decorative colour noise.
