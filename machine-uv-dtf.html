MACHINE GUIDE / UV DTF TRANSFER

UV DTF Transfer System

A transfer-production route for qualified hard-surface applications, labels, stickers and decorative object graphics.

WHAT THE SYSTEM DOES

Understand the machine before choosing the route.

UV DTF prints a graphic onto transfer film, builds the required ink and adhesive layers, and transfers the finished graphic onto a qualified hard surface.

  • Best-considered applicationsSTICKERS, LABELS AND DECALS, bottles, glassware, acrylic pieces, selected metal objects and other reviewed hard-surface applications.
  • Material / surface contextSmooth rigid or semi-rigid surfaces where geometry, surface condition and handling permit a reliable transfer.
  • Validation boundarySurface compatibility, curvature, coating, handling and wear expectations must be reviewed before production. No universal adhesion claim is made.
